What are common plumbing problems that occur at home?

Common issues include leaking taps or pipes, clogged drains, low water pressure, running toilets, and water heater problems. These are usually caused by wear and tear, blockages, or faulty fittings and can be resolved quickly with professional service.

How much does it cost to fix a plumbing problem in my home?

The cost of fixing a plumbing problem in your home can vary depending on the type of problem and the hourly rate of the plumber. It's best to get a quote from the plumber before the work begins.

How long does it take to repair a plumbing problem?

Most basic plumbing services take 30 to 90 minutes depending on the issue. Complex repairs or replacements may take longer based on the work involved.

Is there a warranty on plumbing services?

Yes, a 30-day service warranty is provided on workmanship. It covers the quality of the repair performed during the service. This warranty does not apply to external damage, normal wear and tear, or issues related to customer-provided materials or parts.
