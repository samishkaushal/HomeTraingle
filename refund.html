Introduction

This Refund and Cancellation Policy ("Policy") governs the terms under which customers may cancel home service bookings and request refunds for payments made through or outside the Home Triangle Platform.

This Policy forms an integral part of the Platform's Terms of Use. By using the Platform to book home services, you agree to be bound by the provisions contained in this Policy.

1. Payment Made via the Platform at the Time of Booking

1.1 Cancellation Terms

  • Cancellation more than 4 hours before the scheduled service – Full Refund.
  • Cancellation within 4 hours of the scheduled service – 50% Refund.
  • Cancellation after the Service Professional is en route or has reached the location – No Refund.

1.2 Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be initiated to the original payment method within 5–7 business days, subject to bank or payment gateway processing timelines.

2. Payment Made via the Platform After Completion of Service

2.1 Cancellation Terms

Customers may cancel a booking only before the service has commenced. Once the Service Professional has started or completed the service, cancellation and refund requests will not be accepted.

  • Cancellation before service starts – Allowed.
  • After service commencement – No Cancellation.
  • After service completion – No Refund.

2.2 Dispute Resolution

In case of any genuine service-related dispute, Home Triangle may review the matter and provide an appropriate resolution based on available evidence.

  • Rework by the same Service Professional.
  • Replacement Service Professional.
  • Partial Refund (if approved).
  • Decision based on internal investigation.

3. Payment Made Outside the Platform After Completion of Service

Home Triangle is not responsible for payments made directly to Service Professionals outside the official Platform.

Refunds, cancellations, customer support and dispute resolution are available only for transactions processed through official Home Triangle payment channels.

Important Notice

Customers are strongly advised to make all payments through the Home Triangle Platform to remain eligible for refund protection and customer support.

4. Partial or Advance Payment via Platform Before or During the Service

4.1 Cancellation Before Service Start

Customers who have paid an advance amount through the Home Triangle Platform may request cancellation before the scheduled service starts.

  • Cancellation more than 4 hours before service time – 100% Refund of Advance Amount.
  • Cancellation within 4 hours of service time – 50% Refund of Advance Amount.
  • Cancellation after service has started – Refund eligibility will be decided after Platform assessment.

4.2 Mid-Service Cancellation

If the Customer cancels after the Service Professional has started the work, the refund amount (if any) will depend upon the actual work completed.

  • Refund calculated on a pro-rata basis.
  • Cost of materials already used will be deducted.
  • Labour charges already incurred may be deducted.
  • Final refund will be decided after Platform assessment.

Approved refunds will normally be initiated within 5–7 business days, subject to internal review and bank processing timelines.

5. Partial or Advance Payment Made Outside the Platform

Home Triangle bears no responsibility for advance or partial payments made directly to Service Professionals outside the official Platform.

Such payments are not covered under this Refund & Cancellation Policy, and no refund, customer support or dispute resolution will be provided.

Customer Advisory

Always make payments through the official Home Triangle website or mobile application to remain eligible for refunds and customer protection.

6. Valid Mode of Cancellation & Refund Request

All cancellation and refund requests must be submitted only through the official Home Triangle website or mobile application.

Requests made directly to Service Professionals or through any third-party communication channel will not be considered valid.

  • Official Website
  • Home Triangle Mobile App
  • Customer Support Ticket

7. General Provisions

The following provisions apply to all refund and cancellation requests submitted through the Home Triangle Platform. These provisions are intended to ensure fairness, transparency and timely resolution of customer concerns.

7.1 Service Provider No-Show

If a confirmed Service Professional cancels the booking or fails to arrive at the scheduled service location, the Customer will be eligible for a full refund of any amount paid through the Platform.

  • 100% refund of advance payment.
  • No cancellation charges.
  • Option to reschedule the service.
  • Replacement Service Professional (subject to availability).

7.2 Duplicate or Erroneous Transactions

If a Customer is charged more than once for the same booking or an incorrect payment is processed through the Platform, Home Triangle will review the transaction.

After successful verification, the excess amount will be refunded to the original payment method.

  • Duplicate UPI Payment
  • Duplicate Card Payment
  • Incorrect Amount Charged
  • Technical Payment Failure

7.3 Refund Processing Timeline

Once a refund request is approved, Home Triangle will initiate the refund within 5–7 business days.

The actual credit of funds depends upon the processing time of your bank, payment gateway or card issuer.

Note

Home Triangle shall not be responsible for delays caused by banks, payment gateways or financial institutions after the refund has been initiated.

7.4 Dispute Escalation

Customers who are dissatisfied with the refund decision may raise a dispute with Home Triangle Customer Support.

All disputes should be reported within 24 hours of the scheduled service time along with supporting evidence wherever applicable.

  • Booking ID
  • Payment Receipt
  • Photos (if applicable)
  • Relevant Communication

8. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

This Refund & Cancellation Policy shall be governed and interpreted in accordance with the laws of the Republic of India.

Any dispute arising out of this Policy shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts located in Bengaluru, Karnataka.

9. Amendments to this Policy

Home Triangle reserves the right to update, modify or replace this Refund & Cancellation Policy at any time without prior notice.

Customers are encouraged to review this Policy periodically. Continued use of the Platform after any modification constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
